# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be someone others can work with. Disagree about the work, not about the person. Assume the
other party is trying to get it right.

## Our standards

**Expected:**

- Critique the code, the design or the argument — never the contributor.
- Give findings that name a location and a consequence. "This is wrong" is not feedback;
  "this closes the ticket with an unresolved blocking finding, here is the path" is.
- Accept a review that contradicts you. This project keeps contradicting verdicts on the
  record precisely because they are informative, and that applies to people too.
- Say what you did not verify. Honesty about the limits of your own work is a contribution.
- Assume good faith across languages and time zones. Most of this project is written by
  people working in their second language.

**Not acceptable:**

- Personal attacks, insults or demeaning comments.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or persistent re-litigation of a settled decision.
- Dismissing a contribution because of who or what produced it, rather than what it contains.

## On AI contributions

Nodera is built on the premise that participants are judged by what they do, not by what kind
of participant they are. That principle applies to this project's own contributors:

- **A contribution is judged on its content.** An agent-produced pull request is reviewed on
  the same terms as any other — no softer, no harsher.
- **Attribution is required, not optional.** Say when an agent produced a change and name the
  accountable human. Presenting agent output as unaided human work is a conduct issue, not a
  style preference: it removes the reader's ability to calibrate.
- **The accountable human answers for it.** An agent cannot be the end of an escalation, and
  "the model wrote it" is not a response to a finding.
- **Volume is not contribution.** Automated output that has not been read by the person
  submitting it wastes reviewers' time, which is the scarcest resource this project has.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions — and
when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report to **conduct@bitoracle.ai**. Reports are handled confidentially, and you will get an
acknowledgement within 10 working days.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that violate this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behaviour they consider inappropriate.
Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face consequences
determined by the other maintainers.

Responses are proportionate: most issues are resolved with a private word. Escalation —
a warning, a temporary ban, a permanent ban — is for repeated or severe behaviour.
